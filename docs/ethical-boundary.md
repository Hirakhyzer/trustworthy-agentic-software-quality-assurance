# Ethical and Operational Boundary

This repository is an academic software-quality research prototype.

## Intended uses

- controlled software-engineering experiments;
- software quality assurance education;
- benchmarking agentic QA architectures;
- studying human oversight and trust calibration;
- evaluating evidence-grounded quality decisions.

## Out of scope

The prototype is not intended to:

- autonomously approve production releases;
- replace accountable software-quality professionals;
- bypass code review, security review, or organizational controls;
- access or modify third-party systems without authorization;
- make employment or performance decisions about developers;
- treat an AI-generated finding as verified evidence without inspection.

## Human responsibility

A trustworthy SQA system should support, not obscure, responsibility. High-impact decisions should have an identifiable accountable reviewer, and the evidence available to that reviewer should be preserved.

## Data governance

Research datasets should avoid confidential source code, credentials, personal data, or proprietary incident records unless appropriate permission and governance are in place. Public or synthetic scenarios are preferred for reproducible research.

## Research transparency

Negative results, inconsistent decisions, unsupported findings, and human overrides are scientifically important. They should be reported rather than filtered out to make the system appear more capable.
